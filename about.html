I'm Lorenzo Vitali, a junior game designer from Italy. I love analyzing things in great detail, just so I can spend an unhealthy amount of time talking about them

Though people may have (forcibly) listened to my monologues on how videogames are an opportunity to experience a fragment of somebody else's feelings and perspective (be it the characters' or the developers); my words weren't quite as effective as an interactive experience. To convey my point of view, I had to start making games. Now, of course you can't just shoot for the moon straight away. So, I studied:

  • In 2025, I completed a three-year Game Design course at TheSIGN Comics & Arts Academy, in Florence, Italy
  • In 2026, I completed a one-year Music Composition and Sound Design course at Scuola Internazionale di Comics, in Florence, Italy
  • As a student, I've built prototypes and led group projects in both Unity and Godot
  • Because of this, I can: design, script in C# and GDScript, write music, design sound effects, and draw pixel art
  • I can definitely lead a team, but that's weird to say as a junior, so I'm a little bit shy about it

Ultimately, my goal is to create as much as possible. I'm a designer at heart, but I will try my best to add anything I can to my skillset

When designing a game, I tend to look for mechanics and systems, that can act as the missing link between sheer gameplay and the feeling or message the game is trying to convey

In my free time, I mainly play videogames or try way too hard at cosplay and makeup. Some games I recommend are: Anodyne 2: Return to Dust, Undertale, Super Paper Mario, Pokémon Black/White, The Stanley Parable, Ena: Dream BBQ, and Frog Fractions
