I'm Lorenzo Vitali, a junior game designer from Italy. I love analyzing things in great detail, just so I can spend an unhealthy amount of time explaining them

As a kid, I wanted to give long speeches, but lacked something to talk about. That's when games entered the picture. I've always perceived playing a game, as an opportunity to experience a fragment of somebody else's feelings and point of view, be it the characters' or the developers'. The variety of lenses you can watch reality through when playing games, is something I will never shut up about

I figured monologuing wasn't exactly interactive, though. People may have (forcibly) listened to me for hours on end, but my dissertations could never allow them to feel the way I did. Ultimately, I had to start making my own games. That way, I could truly convey my point of view. Now, of course you can't just shoot for the moon straight away. So, I studied:

  • In 2025, I completed a three-year Game Design course at TheSIGN Comics & Arts Academy, in Florence, Italy
  • In 2026, I completed a one-year Music Composition and Sound Design course at Scuola Internazionale di Comics, in Florence, Italy
  • As a student, I've built prototypes and lead group projects in both Unity and Godot
  • Because of this, I can: design, script in C# and GDScript, write music, design sound effects, and draw pixel art
  • I can definitely lead a team, but that's weird to say as a junior, so I'm a little bit shy about it


Ultimately, my goal is to create as much as possible. I'm a designer at heart, but I will try my best to add anything I can to my skillset

When designing a game, I tend to look for mechanics and systems, that can act as the missing link between sheer gameplay and whatever feeling or message the game is trying to convey

In my free time, I mainly play games or try way too hard at cosplay and makeup. Some games I recommend are: Anodyne 2: Return to Dust, Undertale, Super Paper Mario, Pokémon Black/White, The Stanley Parable, Ena: Dream BBQ, and Frog Fractions
