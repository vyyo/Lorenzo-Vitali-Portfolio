Foreword

The main goal with Long Live The Hamster King, was to focus on designing a combat system, that could truly make the player feel like they were controlling a horde of hamsters. As one of two designers and the project’s programmer, I’ve designed and coded these aspects of the game:

Core

  • Conflict: resource (subject) management and move planning
  • Goal: escaping all the rooms
  • Fighting scientists is required to reach each exit
  • Most moves deplete the subjects supply
  • Without subjects, the king is exposed to attacks and might die (game over)

Combat: Order

  • The player either moves the king or all subjects
  • Hamsters go first, enemies go last
  • Lane order: center, top, bottom

Combat: Lanes

3-lane-combat dynamics:

  • Each enemy targets specific lanes
  • Enemies target the king only when their target lanes have no subjects
  • Hamsters can attack every lane

Combat: Enemies

Enemy target/attack logic:

  • Base: parallel lane first, adjacent lane(s) second
  • Aoe: parallel lane and all adjacent lanes, simultaneously
  • Heavy: lane with the highest hp subject
  • Sniper: always targets the king
  • Tank: same as Base (stronger)
  • Swapper: swaps lanes with an enemy, then same as base

Player Combat

The player’s moves are reminiscent of a hamster’s behavior: self-destructive attacks, frail resources, incentives to cannibalism and reproduction

Combat: King

The king can:

  • Attack: light pierce damage
  • Cannibalism: eats a subject. Heals equal to subject hp
  • Breed: if the breed bar is full,births 4-12 subjects. All subjects on the field have a 50% death chance

Combat: Subjects 1

  • At the start of a turn, each empty lane is filled by a new subject
  • Filling a lane reduces subjects by 1
  • Subjects can only be increased by using Breed
  • Subjects start out with 1 hp, 0 attack and 0 defense

Combat: Subjects 2

Subjects can:

  • Inject: attack after getting injected, inflicting a variety of bonus damages and status effects. Take 1 damage after attacking
  • Cannibalism: eat up to 9 subjects, increasing hp and attack by 1 per subject
  • Guard: passes the turn to the next subject

Combat: Inject

The subject gets injected with one of four experimental drugs:

  • Flammable: +1 attack, inflicts flammable status
  • Rabies: +5 attack
  • Virus: +2 attack, inflicts virus status (1 damage per turn)
  • Static: +2 attack, inflicts static status, 6 pierce damage on a flammable enemy

Prototyping Tasks

  • Programming (GDScript)
  • Blockout
  • Environment art
